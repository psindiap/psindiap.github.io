Partha Sarathi Purkayastha

M.Sc. Computer Science at ETH Zürich

I am a master’s student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich and a Research Assistant at SCAI Lab, working with Diego Paez-Granados. My research interests span AI in education and healthcare, human–AI interaction, and reliable agentic systems. I am particularly interested in how AI systems reason about context, make decisions, and support people.

At ETH’s LRE Lab, I worked with Mrinmaya Sachan and Heejin Do on dynamic student simulation and the evaluation of adaptive LLM tutors. My current work at SCAI uses multimodal wearable and clinical data for cardiac-recovery monitoring.

Previously, I was a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, working with Akshay Nambi on contextual privacy and LLM summarization. I received my B.E. in Computer Science from BITS Pilani in 2024, graduating with the Director’s Gold Medal and a 9.83/10 CGPA.
