I am a master’s student in Computer Science at **[ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/)** and a Research Assistant at **[SCAI Lab](https://scai.ethz.ch/)**, working with **[Diego Paez-Granados](https://scai.ethz.ch/TheLab/people/diego-paez-granados.html)**. My research interests span **AI in education and healthcare, human–AI interaction, and reliable agentic systems**. I am particularly interested in how AI systems reason about context, make decisions, and support people.

At **[ETH’s LRE Lab](https://lre.inf.ethz.ch/)**, I worked with **[Mrinmaya Sachan](https://inf.ethz.ch/people/person-detail.MjYyNzc4.TGlzdC8zMDQsLTg3NDc3NjI0MQ==.html)** and **[Heejin Do](https://doheejin.github.io/)** on dynamic student simulation and the evaluation of adaptive LLM tutors. My current work at SCAI uses multimodal wearable and clinical data for cardiac-recovery monitoring.

Previously, I was a Research Fellow at **[Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/)**, working with **[Akshay Nambi](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/akshayn/)** on contextual privacy and LLM summarization. I received my B.E. in Computer Science from **[BITS Pilani](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/)** in 2024, graduating with the **Director’s Gold Medal** and a **9.83/10 CGPA**.
